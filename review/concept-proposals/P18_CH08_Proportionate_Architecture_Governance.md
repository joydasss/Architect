# Concept Proposals: Part 18 CH08

| Concept | Proposed Owner | Definition / Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Risk-proportionate architecture assurance | Part 18 CH08 | An assurance approach that scales review depth and authority to impact, irreversibility, uncertainty, regulatory exposure, and cross-domain reach, using explicit escalation triggers and reusable evidence. |
| Time-bound architecture exception | Part 18 CH08 | A scoped deviation from a principle or standard with rationale, compensating controls, accountable owner, expiry or reassessment trigger, and a defined disposition path. |

# Concept Proposals: Part 17 CH02

| Concept | Proposed Owner | Definition / Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Constraint-first AI capability selection | Part 17 CH02 | A selection method that separates hard workload constraints from preferences, evaluates candidates against shared task evidence, and chooses the least complex deployment that satisfies the constraints. |
| Capability-preserving fallback | Part 17 CH02 | A degraded path that preserves an explicit user-facing contract through deterministic processing, human handling, asynchronous completion, or a declared unavailable state when the primary model path cannot safely complete. |

# Concept Proposals: Part 18 CH06

| Concept | Proposed Owner | Definition / Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Enterprise data-domain ownership contract | Part 18 CH06 | An agreement naming the accountable business owner, authoritative source, semantic definition, quality expectations, access and permitted-use rules, consumer change notice, and escalation path for a data domain or product. |
| Authority-at-attribute model | Part 18 CH06 | A governance approach that identifies which system or owner is authoritative for a specific entity or attribute, allowing multiple systems to own distinct fields without ambiguous conflict resolution. |

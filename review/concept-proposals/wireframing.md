## Notes for the merge step

- This chapter intentionally recaps and links, rather than re-teaches, Problem
  Framing and Journey Mapping outputs, preserving ownership boundaries already
  registered in [CORE_CONCEPTS.md](../../CORE_CONCEPTS.md).
- Rapid Prototyping is treated as a sibling owner of prototype-cycle strategy.
  This chapter positions wireframes as an upstream structural artifact and does
  not claim prototype-iteration method ownership.
- Usability Testing is not assumed to exist yet. The chapter includes only
  wireframe test-readiness checks and defers full evaluation-method depth to
  that future chapter.
- [PART_OVERVIEW.md](../../02_Design_Thinking_and_Product_Management/PART_OVERVIEW.md)
  currently lists Wireframing as Not Started; this session did not edit plan
  or status files per chapter-author constraints.

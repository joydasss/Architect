# Concept Proposals: Part 17 CH05

| Concept | Proposed Owner | Definition / Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Identity-bound retrieval | Part 17 CH05 | A retrieval architecture in which trusted principal, purpose, and entitlement context constrain candidate selection before evidence is supplied to a model, with explicit treatment of caches and derived copies. |
| Citation provenance chain | Part 17 CH05 | A traceable relationship from a returned claim and citation through request context and retrieved passage to a versioned source location, enabling inspection without asserting that retrieval alone proves correctness. |

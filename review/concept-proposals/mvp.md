| Concept | Suggested Owning Chapter | Scope note |
|---|---|---|
| MVP Taxonomy (Concierge, Wizard of Oz, Piecemeal, Landing Page, Single-Feature) | [MVP](../../02_Design_Thinking_and_Product_Management/CH23_MVP.md) | Formal five-type operational taxonomy of Minimum Viable Products, categorizing each by technical mechanism, human-in-the-loop overhead, evidence fidelity, and optimal risk context |
| Architectural Strategy for Throwaway vs. Evolutionary MVPs | [MVP](../../02_Design_Thinking_and_Product_Management/CH23_MVP.md) | Decision criteria and technical boundaries between disposable prototypes/facades and production-grade evolutionary foundations, including migration paths and tech-debt containment |
| Riskiest Assumption Testing (RAT) | [MVP](../../02_Design_Thinking_and_Product_Management/CH23_MVP.md) | Methodology for isolating, prioritizing, and falsifying critical value, feasibility, and viability assumptions prior to full feature development |
| Quality and Security-Preserving Scope Reduction ("Vertical Slice" Model) | [MVP](../../02_Design_Thinking_and_Product_Management/CH23_MVP.md) | Slicing strategy for reducing feature breadth without compromising non-functional quality attributes, security boundaries, or data integrity |
| Post-Launch Pivot vs. Persevere Decision Engine | [MVP](../../02_Design_Thinking_and_Product_Management/CH23_MVP.md) | Structured quantitative and qualitative evaluation engine for determining whether to iterate, re-architect, pivot (zoom-in, zoom-out, customer, engine of growth), or terminate based on MVP telemetry |

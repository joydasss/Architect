# Concept Proposals: Part 17 CH14

| Concept | Proposed Owner | Definition / Scope |
|---|---|---|
| AI cost per completed outcome | Part 17 CH14 | A workload unit-economics measure that includes model, data, network, storage, idle capacity, retries, human correction, and failure costs per accepted business outcome. |
| AI cost-quality frontier | Part 17 CH14 | The set of non-dominated workload architectures that satisfy hard quality and policy floors while trading off cost, latency, throughput, and sustainability under explicit assumptions. |

# Concept Proposals: Part 17 CH12

| Concept | Proposed Owner | Definition / Scope |
|---|---|---|
| AI governance control plane | Part 17 CH12 | The system of versioned policies, accountable approvals, exceptions, evidence, and reassessment triggers that governs AI use across data, model, tool, deployment, and operational boundaries. |
| Model-output trust boundary | Part 17 CH12 | A boundary that treats model-generated text and tool proposals as untrusted input and requires deterministic validation, identity-bound authorization, and controlled execution before state changes. |

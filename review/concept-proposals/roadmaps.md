# Concept Proposals — Roadmaps (CH21)

Chapter: [Roadmaps](../../02_Design_Thinking_and_Product_Management/CH21_Roadmaps.md)
· Part 02, Product Management subsection.

Per the Concept Ownership Rule in
[STYLE_GUIDE.md](../../STYLE_GUIDE.md), these are proposed additions to
[CORE_CONCEPTS.md](../../CORE_CONCEPTS.md), not yet merged. All are new
cross-cutting concepts this chapter introduces and fully teaches; suggested
ownership is this chapter (Roadmaps) in every case.

| Concept | Suggested Owning Chapter | Scope note |
|---|---|---|
| Roadmap format taxonomy (outcome-based / feature-based / timeline-based) | [Roadmaps](../../02_Design_Thinking_and_Product_Management/CH21_Roadmaps.md) | What each format actually commits to (result vs. deliverable vs. deliverable+date) and why outcome-based is the generally preferred default |
| "Roadmap as promise" anti-pattern | [Roadmaps](../../02_Design_Thinking_and_Product_Management/CH21_Roadmaps.md) | The specific mechanism by which a timeline-based roadmap's probabilistic estimate is stripped of its confidence framing as it moves through sales/contracts and becomes a contractual-liability broken commitment |
| Now/Next/Later horizon format | [Roadmaps](../../02_Design_Thinking_and_Product_Management/CH21_Roadmaps.md) | Qualitative, non-calendar roadmap presentation format; explicitly distinguished from Product Strategy's Three Horizons model (orthogonal axes: communication-certainty-by-quarter vs. funding-risk-by-year), not a re-labeling of it |
| Roadmap confidence/certainty decay by horizon | [Roadmaps](../../02_Design_Thinking_and_Product_Management/CH21_Roadmaps.md) | Principle that reliability of a roadmap claim decreases with horizon distance and must be communicated explicitly (qualifier/visual treatment), not left uniform across near- and far-term items |
| Strategy-to-roadmap contract construction pipeline | [Roadmaps](../../02_Design_Thinking_and_Product_Management/CH21_Roadmaps.md) | The concrete six-step mechanism (intake contract triple -> format -> horizon placement -> non-goal attachment -> confidence qualifier -> audience-view rendering) that fulfills Product Strategy's Section 4.7 forward reference |
| Audience-tiered roadmap communication (single-source, multiple-views model) | [Roadmaps](../../02_Design_Thinking_and_Product_Management/CH21_Roadmaps.md) | Deriving exec/sales, engineering, and customer views from one internal source-of-truth roadmap record rather than independently hand-maintained per-audience documents |
| Tiered external roadmap disclosure policy | [Roadmaps](../../02_Design_Thinking_and_Product_Management/CH21_Roadmaps.md) | Concrete, written horizon-stability-based lag rules for what may appear in a customer-facing roadmap and when; fulfills Product Strategy's Section 13 forward reference ("controlled external roadmap disclosure") |
| Roadmap governance and revision cadence (routine vs. event-triggered) | [Roadmaps](../../02_Design_Thinking_and_Product_Management/CH21_Roadmaps.md) | Standing process distinguishing scheduled roadmap review from immediate, disclosure-tier-scaled revision triggered by a broken assumption |

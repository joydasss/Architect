# Concept Proposals: Part 17 CH10

| Concept | Proposed Owner | Definition / Scope |
|---|---|---|
| AI release manifest | Part 17 CH10 | An immutable description of the behavior-changing artifacts and policy versions composing a deployed AI capability, linked to provenance, evaluation evidence, deployment identity, and rollback target. |
| AI capability retirement contract | Part 17 CH10 | A controlled process for inventorying consumers, migrating or constraining them, disabling endpoints and credentials, and separately satisfying provenance-retention and data-deletion obligations. |

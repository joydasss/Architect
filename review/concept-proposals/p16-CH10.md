| Transformer serving cost model (parameter count, FLOPs per token, KV-cache bytes per token, decode as memory-bound) | [Transformers and Attention](../../16_AI_and_GenAI/CH10_Transformers_and_Attention.md) | Closed-form formulas for weight memory, `2N` forward and `6ND` training FLOPs, `2*L*g*d_h*b` cache bytes per token, and the bandwidth bound on decode. | Three meanings of context length (architectural, trained, effective) and the position-sweep test | [Transformers and Attention](../../16_AI_and_GenAI/CH10_Transformers_and_Attention.md) | A model-card context window is not evidence of usable length; effective length is measured per task by placing the needed evidence at varied depths. CH15, CH17, and CH18 recap it for prompt and retrieval design. |
| Model contract for a checkpoint (position scheme and base, norm placement, head counts, tied embeddings, special tokens, numeric formats) | [Transformers and Attention](../../16_AI_and_GenAI/CH10_Transformers_and_Attention.md) | Architecture settings recorded and checked at deployment, with logit-tolerance and cache-equivalence regression tests; a silent mismatch yields fluent but degraded output. CH29 recaps it for release gating. |
| Attention weights are not explanations | [Transformers and Attention](../../16_AI_and_GenAI/CH10_Transformers_and_Attention.md) | Weights multiply value vectors and mix across heads and layers, so heat maps are debugging aids only; use ablation, attribution, and counterfactual tests for claims about inputs. CH24 and CH31 recap it for explanation and governance. |
| Tenant isolation in batched transformer inference (masks, cache blocks, prefix-cache keys, routing capacity) | [Transformers and Attention](../../16_AI_and_GenAI/CH10_Transformers_and_Attention.md) | Cross-request leakage paths created by shared batches and caches, with a two-tenant marker test. Part 17 owns the system-level isolation architecture. |

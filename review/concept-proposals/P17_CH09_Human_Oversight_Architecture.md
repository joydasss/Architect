# Concept Proposals: Part 17 CH09

| Concept | Proposed Owner | Definition / Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Operational human-oversight contract | Part 17 CH09 | A workflow agreement specifying the human's authority, timing, evidence, expected response, capacity, escalation path, and intervention mechanism for an AI-assisted decision. |
| Actionable AI override | Part 17 CH09 | An override mechanism that can cancel or correct a pending or completed workflow action, or contain and remediate an irreversible effect, while preserving the original decision evidence and accountable owner. |

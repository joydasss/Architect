# Concept Proposals - Ideation chapter

Staged for merge into [CORE_CONCEPTS.md](../../CORE_CONCEPTS.md). Do not edit
CORE_CONCEPTS.md directly from this chapter's session.

| Concept | Proposed Owner | Scope note |
|---|---|---|
| Divergent vs. convergent ideation cycle | Ideation chapter | Operational separation of generation and evaluation phases, including phase-purity facilitation controls and cadence |
| Ideation facilitation mechanics | Ideation chapter | Workshop mechanics: silent generation, equalized participation, anti-dominance controls, and phase-transition governance |
| Ideation method families | Ideation chapter | Taxonomy of idea-generation methods (association, constraint, inversion, analogy, morphological, rapid visual ideation) and when to use each |
| Idea selection funnel (signal -> criteria -> portfolio) | Ideation chapter | Structured narrowing from raw idea set to hypothesis-ready candidate portfolio with explicit scoring dimensions |
| Anti-premature-convergence practice | Ideation chapter | Detection and prevention of early lock-in, including anchoring and authority-bias countermeasures in ideation rooms |

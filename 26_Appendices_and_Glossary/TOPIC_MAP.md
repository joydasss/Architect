# Part 26 — Appendices and Glossary Topic Map

This map defines the reference and navigation material planned for Part 26. These contents support consistent terminology, lookup, and book navigation; they are not substitutes for the explanatory chapters that own each concept.

## Part-wide ownership boundaries

- Part 26 owns consolidated reference indexes and assembled navigation aids.
- Domain chapters remain authoritative for definitions, technical explanations, and recommendations; appendix entries should point readers back to those owners where possible.
- Keep terminology consistent with first-use expansions in the main text, preserve aliases, and make indexes useful for both readers and maintainers.

## Cross-part stakeholders and scenarios

- Readers need quick lookup while studying or applying an architecture concept; editors need stable, maintainable reference structures as chapters change.
- Important scenarios include finding an acronym from its expansion, locating a concept from a chapter, following a cross-part dependency, and updating navigation after a chapter is moved.

## Contents

### CH01 Glossary of Terms

**Reader outcome:** Find concise, consistent definitions of key architecture terms with pointers to fuller explanations.
**Coverage:** Canonical term, definition, context, aliases, disambiguation, cross-references, and owning part/chapter.
**Diagram opportunities:** (1) term-to-owner cross-reference graph; (2) concept relationships by domain; (3) synonymous and overloaded term map; (4) glossary entry lifecycle; (5) reader lookup path from term to chapter.

### CH02 Acronym Index

**Reader outcome:** Expand acronyms quickly and locate their first-use explanation and authoritative context.
**Coverage:** Acronym, expanded form, domain, first-use location, alternate forms, and ambiguous acronym notes.
**Diagram opportunities:** (1) acronym-to-expansion lookup flow; (2) acronym clusters by part; (3) ambiguous acronym disambiguation tree; (4) first-use trace across chapters; (5) acronym maintenance workflow.

### CH03 Technology Index

**Reader outcome:** Find technologies and standards discussed in the handbook and navigate to the relevant architectural context.
**Coverage:** Technology name, category, use context, version-sensitive notes where needed, and chapter references.
**Diagram opportunities:** (1) technology-to-capability map; (2) technology category hierarchy; (3) references by architecture layer; (4) technology lifecycle and version-sensitivity markers; (5) lookup route from technology to decision context.

### CH04 Further Reading / Bibliography

**Reader outcome:** Follow authoritative and useful sources that extend the handbook's explanations.
**Coverage:** Bibliographic metadata, source quality, topic association, publication details, stable URLs or identifiers, and citation consistency.
**Diagram opportunities:** (1) source-to-topic coverage map; (2) reading path by topic and depth; (3) source authority and recency dimensions; (4) citation-to-chapter trace; (5) bibliography update and link-check workflow.

### CH05 Combined Table of Contents

**Reader outcome:** Navigate the assembled handbook by part, chapter, and hierarchy with reliable links.
**Coverage:** Canonical part order, chapter titles, heading hierarchy, anchors, generated/manual boundaries, and link validation.
**Diagram opportunities:** (1) book hierarchy tree; (2) part dependency and reading-order map; (3) chapter-to-anchor link structure; (4) source overview to combined contents flow; (5) navigation build and validation pipeline.
